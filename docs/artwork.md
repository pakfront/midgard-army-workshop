# Make standee artwork

Choose the assembled base first. The workshop repeats or mirrors the supplied image to build the strip, so draw one upright per PNG.

| Unit | Draw in one image | Default assembled base |
|---|---|---|
| Foot | One frontal rank. Use 7–8 varied figures for light troops and normally 4–6 for skirmishers. | 40 × 20 mm |
| Cavalry | One complete rider and mount in strict side profile. The workshop repeats the rider across the frontage. | 40 × 20 mm |
| Hero | One complete foot figure or one mounted profile, on a single upright. | 20 × 20 mm |
| Monstrosity | One or two large monsters, or one war machine. Choose a descriptive unit name and a fixed panel height. | 40 × 20 mm |

Cavalry and Monstrosities also support 30 or 40 mm base depth. Monster panel heights and upright counts stay fixed. The Giant uses one 35 mm panel. The paired Trolls and paired Ogres each share one 23 mm panel.

## Build a generation or drawing brief

Use [Orc Axes](../artwork/middle-earth-third-age/Orcs/orc-axemen-clean.png) for the print style and an existing unit from the intended faction for palette and equipment. Describe the figure count, pose, view and intended print width. This example is for a light Foot unit:

> Draw eight frontal militia spearmen in a single horizontal rank for a 40 mm paper miniature. Vary head heights, clothing, shields and stances. Use bold dark contours, broad flat colors, two or three large shading tones and simple faces. Keep the spear tips, boots and shields inside the frame. Leave a narrow transparent margin on every edge. Omit ground, shadows, text, borders, hatching, tiny fittings and surface texture.

For skirmishers, leave visible gaps and mix poses such as standing, kneeling, aiming and moving. Use skirmisher density even for lightly equipped troops. [Gondor Rangers](../artwork/middle-earth-third-age/Gondor/gondor-rangers.png) shows six figures. For cavalry, specify one left-facing horse or other mount with ears, tail, hooves and weapon tip in frame. See the [Gondor lancer profile](../artwork/middle-earth-third-age/Gondor/gondor-lancer-profile.png).

## Prepare the PNG

Export a transparent RGBA PNG. Check the silhouette on light and dark backgrounds for a baked-in backdrop, stray pixels and clipped weapons. Trim excess empty space while retaining a small clear margin all around. At print size, faces and heraldry should still read as simple shapes. The workshop supplies the reverse, ground color and fold guides.

Save the approved image under the matching era and faction, then follow [Add a catalog unit](adding-units.md) to register and print it.
